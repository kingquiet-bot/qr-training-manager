QR Based Training Manager

Seamless Attendance for Hybrid Learning

By Ye' Naung

Step 1: Setup & Import

  • Setup Event[cite: 2]
  • Import Attendance List[cite: 2]
  • Generate QR for Imported Attendance List[cite: 2]

Step 2: Distribution

Attendee can get the QR from Telegram Bot[cite: 2].

Step 3: Live Check-in

In Registration Time (30 mins before class), switch status to "Live"[cite: 2].

  • In-Person: Scan the Attendee's QR for Attendance Record[cite: 2].
  • Online: Self-check-in with ID via link provided in meeting[cite: 2].

Step 4: Control & Reports

  • Switch status to "Close" so no one can scan or self-check-in[cite: 2].
  • When class ends, Event Employee Can Download Attendance Report[cite: 2].
  • Send Attendance Report to the desired email address[cite: 2].
